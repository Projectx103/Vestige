Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy was drafted to describe how Vestige actually operates. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and doesn't constitute legal advice — before relying on it for full compliance with Philippine data privacy law (the Data Privacy Act of 2012) or any registration requirements with the National Privacy Commission, have it reviewed by a qualified professional.

What We Collect

When you create a Vestige Member account, we collect:

  • Your email address, first and last name
  • Your shopping preference (Men's/Women's)
  • Your date of birth
  • Bidding activity — items you've bid, mined, stolen, or locked, and your favorites list
  • Any screenshots or details you send us directly via Facebook Messenger to confirm a bid

If you don't create an account and instead message us directly on Facebook, we only have whatever information Facebook provides to page admins and whatever you share with us in that conversation.

How We Use It

  • To create and manage your Member account
  • To confirm and process your bids and orders
  • To send you updates about products, offers, and Member benefits — only if you opted in at signup
  • To contact you about an order, a bid confirmation, or an account issue

Where It's Stored

Account and product data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Product and profile images are stored using Cloudinary. Both are third-party infrastructure providers — we don't operate our own servers.

Payment & Order Confirmation

Vestige does not process payments through this website. Bidding, payment (typically GCash or cash on delivery within Metro Manila), and order confirmation currently happen through direct communication on our Facebook page. We do not store your payment details.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your account information at any time by messaging us on Facebook. If you no longer want to receive marketing emails, you can opt out by contacting us directly — we don't yet have a self-service unsubscribe link built into the site.

Cookies & Tracking

This site does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Firebase Authentication uses browser storage to keep you signed in on your device.

Children's Privacy

Vestige Membership is intended for users who are at least 13 years old. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Vestige grows. Significant changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of the page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Message us directly on our Facebook page.

What Vestige Is

Vestige Thrift Shop is a curated secondhand and thrifted apparel business based in the Philippines. Every item listed is one-of-one — once claimed, that exact piece is no longer available. We are not affiliated with any of the original brands whose secondhand items we sell (Nike, Adidas, The North Face, etc.) — those are the original manufacturers' trademarks, used here only to accurately describe the secondhand goods themselves.

How Bidding Works

  • Mine places the next bid at the item's current price increment.
  • Steal lets you outbid the current highest bidder on that item.
  • Lock instantly reserves the item for you at the admin-set lock price, ending bidding on that item immediately.
  • Regardless of which action you take, you must send a screenshot of your bid and relevant details to our Facebook page to confirm it. A bid is not considered final until we confirm it.
  • We reserve the right to void bids that appear to be made in bad faith (e.g. bidding with no intent to complete the purchase).

Accounts

Creating a Vestige Member account is optional. It lets you bid faster, save favorites, and generate bid-confirmation images automatically. You do not need an account to buy from us — you can bid and purchase entirely through Facebook Messenger.

You're responsible for keeping your account details accurate. We may suspend accounts used for spam, fraud, or repeated bad-faith bidding.

Payment & Fulfillment

Payment is currently handled directly through our Facebook page (typically GCash or cash on delivery within Metro Manila). Once a bid is confirmed and payment received, we prepare and ship or arrange handoff of your item. Shipping timelines and fees are communicated directly through Facebook.

Item Condition

All items are secondhand/thrifted. We describe condition and any flaws honestly in each listing's photos and description. Given the nature of thrifted goods, minor signs of prior use should be expected unless the listing states otherwise.

Returns

Because each item is one-of-one, returns are handled on a case-by-case basis — message us directly if there's a genuine issue with an item you received (e.g. it doesn't match its listed condition).

Limitation of Liability

Vestige is a small, independently run thrift business. We do our best to describe items accurately and fulfill orders promptly, but we're not liable for indirect damages, delays outside our control (e.g. courier issues), or losses beyond the value of the item purchased.
